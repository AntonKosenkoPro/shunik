Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026.

What Lifio collects

  • Account email. Lifio signs you in with a one-time email code or with Sign in with Apple. Your email address identifies your account on the relay server.
  • Time entries you create. Entry names, categories, notes, and start/end times — the content you track. Entries sync to the relay so your devices stay consistent.
  • Device identifier. A random per-device ID keeps each device's login session separate.
  • Apple identity reference. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides a stable identifier so you return to the same account.

Where your data lives

  • On your device first. Entries, categories, and the running timer live in a per-account database on your iPhone and work fully offline.
  • On the relay server. Your synced account, entries, and categories live on the Lifio relay in the United States (N. Virginia): a managed Postgres database as the system of record, fronted by the Lifio API server, so the app can sign you in and sync across devices.

What Lifio never does

  • No tracking, no analytics SDKs, no advertising.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties, except the infrastructure that delivers it (email delivery for login codes, hosting for the relay).

Deletion

You delete data yourself in the app under Profile → Account. The three actions do different things:

  • Sign Out ends the session on this device only. All data — on this device and on the relay — is kept, and signing back in restores it.
  • Delete local data removes only this account's data from this device (entries, categories, pending sync state) and signs you out. Your synced account and other devices are untouched.
  • Delete account and local data permanently deletes your Lifio account and all synced entries, categories, and notes, removes this account's data from this device, and signs out your other devices. If you signed up with Apple, Lifio also automatically unlinks Sign in with Apple authorization; if Apple is momentarily unreachable, unlinking is retried automatically and only a minimal encrypted revocation record is kept until Apple confirms.

Limits worth knowing. Your other devices erase this account's local copy only after they obtain valid confirmation of deletion. An offline device keeps its copy until it reconnects and verifies; if confirmation is unavailable or has expired, the copy stays locked rather than being erased on assumption. A failed sign-in alone never wipes anything. Backups follow a configured 30-day retention policy and expire through scheduled rotation rather than being immediately rewritten. Deletion removes your live data; a backup must not be restored into live service without reconciling account deletions. If you revoke Lifio in your Apple Settings (Apple Account → Sign in with Apple) instead, that only signs you out: your Lifio account, synced data, and local files are preserved. Signing in again with the same login email returns you to the same Lifio account and data.

Security

Traffic between the app and the relay uses TLS. Login codes and Lifio refresh-token authentication records are stored server-side as hashes. An encrypted, temporary refresh-recovery record lets the app recover an interrupted sign-in session without storing its credential in plaintext. Apple tokens needed for automatic authorization revocation are encrypted separately, including minimal pending revocation work after account deletion, and removed when revocation completes. App credentials and deletion-recovery information live in the iOS Keychain on your device.
