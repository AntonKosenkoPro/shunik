Where your time goes.

Lifio is a personal time-tracking app for iOS. Your device is the source of truth — sign in once, then track fully offline while a lightweight relay keeps your devices in sync.

Join the TestFlight beta

How it works

  1. Name it — type what you're doing or tap a recent entry.
  2. Start — the timer counts up while you work.
  3. Stop — the entry saves on your device and syncs when you're online.

Review your history day by day, tag entries with categories, and see the breakdown in Insights.

Private by design

Lifio collects the minimum needed to work: your login email and the time entries you create. No tracking, no analytics, no ads — and your data is never sold. Read the full privacy policy.
